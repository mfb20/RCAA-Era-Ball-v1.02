THE FORMAT

TWO MODES. TWO SIDES. ONE TEAM.

  1. Spin the era. REB randomly selects one season and one team from that season.
  2. Draft one player. Every card carries an offensive and defensive position.
  3. Choose a mode. Classic drafts four starters. Bench drafts five players and lets you bench one on each side.
  4. Set your lineup. REB favors natural positions, but you can select two slots on the same side to swap them.
  5. Play the season. Go 3–3 or better to reach the playoffs. A 5–1 or 6–0 record earns the top seed and a Bowl bye.
QB→WR: −5%. WR→QB: −50%. Rusher↔CB: −8%. MVP boosts the whole team. OPOY and DPOY boost their side even above a displayed 99. Championship and Super Bowl MVP traits improve playoff performance.

You get one total reroll per draft. It replaces both the rolled season and team. The hindsight result uses every roll from each pick, including the team you rerolled away.
